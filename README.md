## About Me

I am an IT Support and Service Desk Analyst based in Ontario, Canada. I completed my Computer Systems Technician – Networking diploma at Georgian College in August 2026, and I also hold a BBA in Project Management from Yorkville University.

For my co-op, I worked as a Tier 1 Service Desk Analyst at Royal Victoria Regional Health Centre (RVH) from January to April 2026, handling about 10 to 12 tickets a day — password resets, Microsoft 365 issues, and account access problems. Before that, I spent two years (May 2022 – May 2024) as a Cable Service & Installation Technician at Intek Communications, working on Rogers services in homes and businesses.

I am currently looking for IT support, service desk, and junior cloud roles in Ontario, and I am open to relocating anywhere in Canada.
